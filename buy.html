The path to the keys

Your home-buying timeline.

Every purchase follows the same general path. Here's what to expect from the first conversation to closing day.

01

Pre-approval

Evaluate savings, credit, and budget to understand your affordability and buying power.

02

Start the search

Begin touring homes that fit your criteria and budget, narrowing down what matters most.

03

Make an offer

Submit a competitive offer with terms aligned to your interests and the current market.

04

Earnest money

Deposit the earnest money agreed in the contract, applied toward what's owed at closing.

05

Home inspection

Have a professional inspect the home for any hidden issues during the due-diligence period.

06

Negotiate repairs

Work through any necessary repairs with the seller before the due-diligence window closes.

07

Appraisal

The lender orders an appraisal to confirm the property's value and condition.

08

Moving preparations

Switch utilities into your name, change your mailing address, and line up a moving company.

09

Final walk-through

Walk the home one last time to make sure you're satisfied with its condition.

10

Closing & possession

Sign the closing paperwork, receive the keys, and step into your new home.
