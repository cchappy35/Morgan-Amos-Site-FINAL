HER STORY
How this began.

Morgan was born and raised right here in Columbus. She graduated from the University of Georgia in the spring of 2015 and earned her Georgia and Alabama real estate licenses that same year.

Over a decade later, she's still doing what she set out to do: helping families across the area buy and sell the homes they live their lives in.

Morgan and her husband Neal, her high school sweetheart, are raising their two daughters, Wells and Ella, just a few miles from the streets she grew up on. Neal is a co-owner of Southern Straws, a Columbus-based cheese straw company. Together, they're building the same kind of life here that they hope to help other families build.

How she works

An honest approach to a personal decision.

Buying or selling a home is one of the biggest decisions most people ever make. Morgan approaches it the same way every time: with honesty, preparation, and a deep familiarity with the Columbus market she's worked in for over a decade.

Whether her clients are listing the home they raised kids in or buying their first one, she brings the same level of care to every transaction.
